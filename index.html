The body as an instrument

Small motion sensors are sewn into a glove. As you move, they feel every turn, swing and flick of your hand and turn it into music.

There are no right notes and no wrong moves. Your body is the instrument; all you have to do is listen to what it plays.

  1. 1

    Wear the glove

    Slip it on so the sensor rests on the back of your hand.

  2. 2

    Connect the power

    Plug the glove into its battery, then hold your hand still for a couple of seconds while it wakes up.

  3. 3

    Move your body and explore

    Flick your hand and a note rings out. With two gloves, flick both at the same moment and they play a chord together.

A very ongoing project, sewn together this morning by Aakanksha Agrawal.
